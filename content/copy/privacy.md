## What we collect

The contact form collects your name, email address, optional phone number, and
whatever you write about your project. The readiness check runs entirely in your
browser and stores nothing unless you choose to send your results to us.

## Why we collect it

To respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the work. We
do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone outside SOSE except where we
need to in order to deliver your project, and never without telling you.

## How long we keep it

Inquiries that do not become projects are kept only as long as they are useful
for follow-up. Project records are kept as long as we are required to keep them.

We have not set a published retention period for each type of record. If you want
to know how long we are holding something of yours, ask and we will tell you.

## Your rights

Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 gives you rights over your personal data,
including access to it and correction of it. To exercise them, email
info@sosengineeringke.com.

This page is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice.

## Analytics and cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Nothing you do on these pages is
tracked or profiled, and the readiness check runs entirely in your browser.

Nothing on this site loads from anywhere else. The typefaces, styles, scripts
and images are all served from this domain, so no third party is contacted when
you read a page, and none of them can see that you were here. The only party
that sees your request is whoever hosts the site.

Note: the typefaces used to be served by Google Fonts, and this page disclosed
that. They are now self-hosted from /fonts/ and the site makes no external
request of any kind, so the disclosure was removed rather than left standing as
something that is no longer true.
