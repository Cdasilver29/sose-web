## What we collect

The contact form collects your name, email address, optional phone number, and
whatever you write about your project. The readiness check runs entirely in your
browser and stores nothing unless you choose to send your results to us.

## Why we collect it

To respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the work. We
do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone outside SOSE except where we
need to in order to deliver your project, and never without telling you.

## How long we keep it

Inquiries that do not become projects are kept only as long as they are useful
for follow-up. Project records are kept as long as we are required to keep them.

## Your rights

Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 gives you rights over your personal data,
including access to it and correction of it. To exercise them, email
info@sosengineeringke.com.
