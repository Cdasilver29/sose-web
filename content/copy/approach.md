## Intro

Eyebrow: Why it always runs this way

A process is not paperwork. It is the order in which decisions get made, and the
order is what stops the expensive ones being made blind. The five steps below are
the same on a three-bedroom house and on a warehouse. What changes is the depth,
not the sequence.

## The process

This page owns the five steps in full. Everywhere else on the site, including the
home page, carries the step names only and links here.

Eyebrow: The five steps
H2: What happens, in order.

The steps run as a horizontal timeline on desktop, on the hexagon markers, with
each step then set out in full below it.

### 1. Discovery
We listen first. Your vision, your site, your budget, your constraints, your
timeline, and what you have already committed to. We walk the site, review any
existing drawings or reports, and identify what is unknown. The deliverable is a
clear statement of what you are actually trying to build and what stands in the
way.

### 2. Planning
Feasibility, costing and approvals mapped before commitments are made. Scope
defined, phasing agreed, budget tested against real current rates rather than
optimistic ones, and the approval path identified so nothing surprises you later.
This is the stage where money gets saved.

### 3. Design
Tailored technical designs that fit the purpose and the ground. Coordination
across disciplines so the drawings agree with each other, with a bill of
quantities that a contractor can actually price without guessing.

### 4. Execution
Managed delivery with quality checks at every stage. Site supervision, materials
verification, progress against programme, variations controlled and documented,
and a single point of accountability. You get regular written reports, not
rumours.

### 5. Delivery
Handover with the documents you need to own the building: as-built drawings,
test certificates, warranties, and a maintenance outline. A snag list closed
properly, not quietly.

## How we keep quality honest

Eyebrow: Quality
H2: How we keep quality honest.

- Written scope before work starts. If it is not in the scope, it gets priced
  before it gets built.
- Materials verified against specification on delivery, not on the invoice.
- Hold points where work stops for inspection before it gets covered up.
- Every variation documented with cost and programme impact before approval.
- Compliance with the standards and consents that apply to your project, planned
  from the start rather than chased at the end.

## Ways to work with us

### Advisory
Short engagements. A second opinion, a feasibility review, a cost sanity check, a
dispute you need an independent technical view on.

### Project-managed delivery
We run the project end to end on your behalf, from planning through handover, and
carry responsibility for programme and budget.

### Full building solutions
Design and construction under one accountable team, for clients who would rather
hold one relationship than five.
