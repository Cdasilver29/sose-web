# Structural Audits and Renovations

Eyebrow: Services
H1: Structural audits and renovations
Standfirst: Professional assessments and upgrades for existing buildings. Know
exactly what a structure can carry, and what it needs next.

## What it covers

An audit answers a specific question: is this building safe for what it is being
asked to do, and what does it need? That question comes up when someone wants to
add a floor, change the use, buy the property, or when cracks appear and nobody
can say whether they matter.

We inspect the structure, assess the condition against what it was designed for
and what it is now carrying, and set out findings in plain language with a
prioritised list of what to do.

Renovation work follows the same logic. Before you upgrade a building, you
establish what you are upgrading.

## What you get

- A visual and, where required, instrumented condition survey
- Assessment of the structure against its current and intended use
- Findings classified by severity, with what is urgent separated from what can
  wait
- A prioritised remedial schedule with indicative costs
- A written report you can give to a bank, a buyer, a tenant or an insurer
- Design of remedial or upgrade works where they are needed

## When you need it

- Cracks, sagging, damp or movement you cannot explain
- You want to add floors or change what the building is used for
- You are buying a property and want to know what you are buying
- Walls or columns were removed by a previous owner
- The building was built without professional supervision

## How the engagement runs

Site visit first. Some buildings are clearly fine and the honest answer is a short
report saying so. Where deeper investigation is needed, we tell you what it costs
and why before doing it. We do not find work that is not there.
