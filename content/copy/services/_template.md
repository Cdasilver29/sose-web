# Service page structure

Every service page uses these blocks in this order. Keep the headings, change
the content.

1. Page header: eyebrow "Services", h1, one-line standfirst
2. What it covers: two or three short paragraphs
3. What you get: a list of concrete deliverables, not adjectives
4. When you need it: three or four situations written as the client would
   recognise them
5. How the engagement runs: brief, honest about what SOSE needs from the client
6. Related: two links, usually another service and a field note
7. CTA band: service-specific, never generic
