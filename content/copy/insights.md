# Field notes: index

The index page for the articles in `content/insights/`. It sells nothing. Each
card carries that article's own frontmatter verbatim (category, title, summary,
reading time) so the card and the article never drift apart. Newest first.

## Intro

Eyebrow: Why these exist
Body: Short, practical notes on the decisions that cost the most money in Kenyan
construction. No sales pitch and nothing gated. If a note saves you an expensive
mistake and you never call us, it has done its job.

## Cards

Eyebrow: The notes

One card per file in `content/insights/`, ordered by `publishedAt`, newest
first. Category as the label, title as the heading, summary as the body,
reading time and date on the last line. Card links to `/insights/[slug]/`.
