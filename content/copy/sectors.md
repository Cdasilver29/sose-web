Standfirst: Different buildings fail in different ways. We adjust the method to
the sector, not the other way round.

### Residential
Homes, apartments and mixed developments. Owner-built houses, rental blocks, and
phased family projects where the budget arrives in stages and the design has to
survive the wait.
What matters most here: realistic costing, honest phasing, and a design that can
be built in the sequence your money actually arrives.

### Commercial
Offices, retail and hospitality. Buildings that have to earn, where every month
of delay has a number attached and fit-out coordination decides whether the
opening date holds.
What matters most here: programme certainty and a services design that does not
fight the tenant fit-out.

### Industrial
Warehouses, workshops and light manufacturing. Long spans, heavy floors, vehicle
movement, and future expansion that should be designed for now rather than
retrofitted later.
What matters most here: floor loading, access and the cost of getting it wrong.

### Institutional
Schools, health facilities, places of worship and community buildings. Usually
funded in tranches, often built while occupied, always accountable to a committee.
What matters most here: safe phasing around users, and documentation a committee
can review.

### Government and public projects
Public works delivered against defined standards and procurement processes, with
the documentation and traceability that public accountability requires.
What matters most here: compliance, records and defensible decisions.

### Infrastructure
Access roads, drainage, water and site works. The unglamorous layer that
determines whether everything built on top of it lasts.
What matters most here: drainage and ground conditions, the two things most often
under-investigated.
