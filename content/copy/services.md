# Services: index

The index page for the five services. It does not sell any one of them; it puts
them in front of the reader and lets them pick the door. Each card carries the
service name and that service's own standfirst, taken verbatim from
`content/copy/services/[slug].md`, so the two never drift apart.

## Cards

Eyebrow: The five
Five cards, one per service, each linking to its page.

- Construction consulting → /services/construction-consulting/
  Expert guidance from project planning to completion, so the expensive
  decisions get made with information instead of optimism.

- Project management → /services/project-management/
  Streamlined execution that keeps timelines and budgets honest, with one person
  accountable for both.

- Building solutions → /services/building-solutions/
  Tailored designs and construction for residential, commercial and industrial
  projects, built to purpose rather than to template.

- Structural audits and renovations → /services/structural-audits/
  Professional assessments and upgrades for existing buildings. Know exactly
  what a structure can carry, and what it needs next.

- Sustainable solutions → /services/sustainable-solutions/
  Eco-friendly practices and modern technologies for green construction.
  Buildings that cost less to run and last longer.
