## The idea

Most construction problems are not construction problems. They are decisions
made too early, too late, or without enough information: a foundation designed
before the soil was understood, a budget agreed before the drawings were
finished, a contractor appointed before the scope was clear.

Sight Over Site Engineering exists to close that gap. We are a Kenyan
construction consulting and solutions company dedicated to transforming ideas
into sustainable, high-quality structures. We provide end-to-end building
solutions, from the first drawing to the final handover, combining innovation,
expertise and efficiency to deliver projects that stand the test of time.

Our mission is to be the trusted partner for all your construction and building
needs.

## Three principles

These three are the spine of the page. Each gets its own band, labelled
Principle one, Principle two and Principle three, in this order.

### See it first
Every avoidable cost on a site was a question nobody asked in an office. We front
load the thinking: site conditions, constraints, sequencing, cost drivers,
approvals. The drawings get harder so the build gets easier.

### Own the ground
A design that works in a brochure does not always work on a specific plot in a
specific county in a specific rainy season. We build for the ground we are
standing on, with the suppliers, contractors and regulators who are actually
available.

### Leave it standing
A building is finished when it is still performing in twenty years. Durability,
maintainability and running cost are design decisions, not afterthoughts.

in the first meeting, not in month four. If a structure is sound, we do not

judgement you cannot trust is worth nothing.
