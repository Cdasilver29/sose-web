Are you ready to break ground?

Eight questions, two minutes. You get an honest read on where your project stands and what to sort out next. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to send it.

Land and permissions

Do you have clean title to the land, in the name that will hold the building?
Have you confirmed what the local authority will allow you to build on this plot?
Do you know which approvals your project needs before work can legally start?

Ground and design

Has the soil been investigated, or are you assuming it is fine?
Do you have complete drawings that a contractor could price without guessing?
Do you know where surface water goes during heavy rain, both on your plot and from your neighbours?

Money and delivery

Is your budget based on current material and labour rates, with a contingency you have not already spent?
Do you have a written scope and a way of controlling variations once work starts?
