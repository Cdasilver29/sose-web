Who we are

Sight, before site.

Every lasting structure is built twice — first with foresight, then with concrete and steel. Sight Over Site Engineering (SOSE) is a Kenyan construction consulting and solutions company built on that idea: get the thinking right, and the building follows.

We provide end-to-end building solutions across Kenya, serving clients from urban developments in the cities to community projects in rural areas — combining innovation, expertise and efficiency to deliver projects that stand the test of time.

Our mission is to be the trusted partner for all your construction and building needs.

What we do

Five services. One standard.

From the first feasibility conversation to the final structural sign-off, every engagement gets the same discipline.

Construction Consulting

Expert guidance from project planning to completion — feasibility, budgeting, compliance and technical direction before a single stone moves.

Project Management

Streamlined execution that keeps timelines and budgets honest — one accountable team coordinating contractors, suppliers and approvals.

Building Solutions

Tailored designs and construction for residential, commercial and industrial projects — built to purpose, not to template.

Structural Audits & Renovations

Professional assessments and upgrades for existing buildings — knowing exactly what a structure can carry, and what it needs next.

Sustainable Solutions

Eco-friendly practices and modern technologies for green construction — buildings that cost less to run and last longer.

Why choose SOSE

Built on the ground we build on.

Construction in Kenya has its own physics — regulations, suppliers, county approvals, soil, weather, timelines. We work inside that reality, not around it.

  • Local expertise. Deep understanding of Kenya's construction landscape.
  • Quality assurance. Enduring compliance to standards and best practices.
  • Client-centered approach. Solutions customised to your vision and your budget.
  • Innovation & technology. Modern tools and techniques for efficient delivery.
  • Trusted partnerships. Strong relationships with suppliers, contractors and regulators.
