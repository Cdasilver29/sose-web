Who we are

Sight over Site.

Every lasting structure is built twice. First with foresight, then with concrete and steel. SOSE is a Kenyan construction consulting and solutions company built on that idea: get the thinking right and the building follows.

Based in Nairobi, and serving countrywide in Kenya, we work from urban developments in the cities to community projects in rural areas.

Our mission is to be the trusted partner for all your construction and building needs.

Why choose SOSE

Built on the ground we build on.

Construction in Kenya has its own physics. Regulations, suppliers, county approvals, soil, weather, timelines. We work inside that reality, not around it.

  • Local expertise. Deep understanding of Kenya's construction landscape.
  • Quality assurance. Enduring compliance to standards and best practices.
  • Client-centered approach. Solutions customised to your vision and your budget.
  • Innovation & technology. Modern tools and techniques for efficient delivery.
  • Trusted partnerships. Strong relationships with suppliers, contractors and regulators.
