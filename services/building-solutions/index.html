Building solutions

Tailored designs and construction for residential, commercial and industrial projects, built to purpose rather than to template.

What it covers

End-to-end delivery under one accountable team. Design, documentation, procurement and construction, coordinated so that the drawings, the bill of quantities and what actually gets built are the same thing.

Tailored means the design starts from your brief, your plot and your budget. A house that has to be built in three phases as money arrives is a different design problem from one built in one go, and pretending otherwise is how projects stall with a roof missing.

What you get

One team, one line of accountability.

  • A design developed from your brief, the site survey and the ground conditions
  • Coordinated drawings across disciplines, checked against each other
  • A bill of quantities a contractor can price without guessing
  • Construction managed against programme and budget
  • Quality checks at defined stages before work is covered up
  • Handover documentation: as-built drawings, certificates, maintenance outline
When you need it

Any of these is a good place to begin.

  • You want one relationship instead of five
  • You are building something with a specific operational requirement
  • Your project has to be delivered in phases
  • You have been through a build before and would rather not repeat it
How the engagement runs

Discovery, planning, design, execution, delivery. The full process is set out on the approach page. What we need from you is decisiveness at the design stage.

Changes are nearly free on paper and expensive in concrete.
